# Adding alternative hod approvers who can also approve at the hod stage apart from the preselected hod during a requisition submission.

- Currently, only a pre-selected HOD can approve a given submitted requisition that requires their approval.
- We want to extend this logic by adding alternative hod's who can approve a given requisition.
- A specific HOD can have more than one alternative HOD.
- Alternative HODs will also be able to see requisitions pending their approval and their requisition histories, and also be able to use the review button in various requisition modals. (users added as alternative HODs will need to added the `hod` role in this case).
- Alternative HODs will also receive approval related emails applicable to them.
- Explore the various requisitions in the project that require a HOD approval to see how this new approach will be implemented.
- Seek clarifications regarding the proposed changes.
- Your critics and recommendations are strongly encouraged.
